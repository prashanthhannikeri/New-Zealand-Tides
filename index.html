New Zealand Tides ("the App") is an iOS application developed by Prashanth Hannikeri ("we", "us", or "our"). The App provides tide forecast information for locations around New Zealand.

The App does not require you to create an account or log in. We do not collect, process, transmit, or store any of the following:

Because we do not collect any personal information from any users, the App is safe for use by people of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions).
